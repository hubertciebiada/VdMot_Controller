## Highlights

Changes since 2.2.13. The full list is at the end of these notes.

- **R2: a positioning that ends with an opening move.** The valve inserts are normally closed and
  have a hysteresis loop of 40..55 pulses, and the direction of the last move picks its branch. A
  valve with `liftCounts` above 0 goes to the open end stop, closes to the target minus the lift
  and opens the lift, so it always stands on the opening branch. In a test on six loops two
  positionings to the same target differed by at most 0.03 l/min with R2 and by at most 0.08 l/min
  without it.
- **Off by default.** `liftCounts` and `floorCounts` of `/api/valves/{n}/flow` are 0 by default,
  and a valve without a lift is positioned as in 2.2.13. A lift is 60..1000 pulses and needs
  `refSide` `open` and a `floorCounts` (the seat of the insert plus a margin) below `strokeCounts`.
- **No target below the seat.** With a lift, a target in l/min or in % whose position lies below
  `floorCounts` + 60 goes to that position, and a target in l/min answers `clipped:true`. Targets
  that land on the position where the valve stands, within 2 pulses, need no move.
- **Every other new target goes through the end stop.** A valve with a lift has no one move; a
  target of 0 still closes at once.
- **A wrong end is caught.** A closing move that meets the closed end stop, or a lift that meets an
  end stop, starts the positioning again from the open end stop once; a second time event 417
  reports step 5 or 7 with the code -3.
- **The flow block shows the closing move.** `ms` and `peak` of a valve with a lift are those of
  its closing move, whose peak shows a sticky valve. `ext.lastMove` is the lift.
- **Downgrade without loss.** NVS `flow` keeps the format of 2.2.13; the lifts and floors are in
  the new key `flowlift`, which 2.2.13 ignores. Back on 2.2.13 every valve runs without a lift.
- **MQTT and Home Assistant** do not change.
- **STM32.** The code is that of 2.2.9; only the version number changes. Flashing the STM is
  optional: it keeps the versions of the two MCUs equal. Coming from 2.2.7 or older, flash it as
  2.2.13 says.

## Known limits

- **R2 did not run on hardware before this release.** It passed the host tests, the mutation gate
  and QEMU. The lift and the floor come from the flowbench measurements of one installation;
  measure the floor of your own inserts before you set one.
- **The flow is calculated, not measured.** The firmware has no flow sensor. The l/min it shows
  comes from the curve at the held position.
- **The dashboard does not show the new members.** `liftCounts` and `floorCounts` are set and read
  through the API.
- **Every new target with a lift goes to the open end stop.** A regulator that changes the target
  every minute moves such a valve through its whole stroke each time; `minIntervalS` and
  `deadbandLpm` limit how often.
- **NC valve inserts only.** The motion rules were derived on normally closed inserts. Nobody
  tested normally open inserts.
