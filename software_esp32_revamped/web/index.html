    ESP firmware update

    Upload an ESP32 application image (.bin). The MD5 is computed here and checked by the device before the new image is activated. The ESP restarts; the STM and the valves keep running. A new image that does not come up healthy is rolled back automatically.

    STM firmware

    Upload an STM32 image (max 512 KiB), then flash it. Valves stop moving while flashing (about a minute); targets are re-sent afterwards.

    ImageSizeVersionCRC32

    Restart and reset

    The firmware never resets the STM on its own, but with jumper X20 fitted an ESP reboot or OTA can reset it through the IO15 strap. Resetting the STM makes every valve recalibrate.

    Service move

    Moves the motor by a number of counts with a current limit, outside the normal target control. Use it to free a stuck valve or to test a head. The result appears as the last move.
